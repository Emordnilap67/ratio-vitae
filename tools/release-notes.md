**New in 2.2:**

Alright, so this one's about making the Budget page yours, plus a couple of fixes that needed doing.

- **Roundups** - tap **+ Add** at the bottom of the Budget page and pick **Roundups**. Every purchase out of your main account rounds up to the next $1, $2, $5 or $10, and the app counts it for you - whether your bank does roundups or not. Turn it **On** and it counts from that moment; turn it **Off** and it stops. Press **Add** and it moves what's counted in one tap
- **Goals** - give it a name and a target if you want one (a vacation, a new phone, whatever). Put money in or take it out with **Move money**
- **Savings pots** - same idea, just no target. Rainy day money and whatnot
- **New categories** - make your own category right from **+ Add**, with an icon and a colour
- **Edit list** - move any row on the Budget page up or down, or hide what you don't need. Tap **Done** when you're finished
- **Credit score on the Money widget** - your Equifax and TransUnion scores, how much they moved, and a **+ New score** button
- **Refresh button** - top right, reloads everything. It fades away once you scroll so it's not sitting on your numbers
- **Calmer buttons** - the + and the Add button got toned down. It's a budgeting app, not a candy store
- **One colour per title** - every sheet's title is one solid colour now instead of the rainbow
- **Fixed: picking several statements** - only the first one used to go in, now they all do
- **Fixed: a statement's balance** - it adds what went on the card after the statement and takes off what you paid since
- **Fixed: logging pills for yesterday** - it didn't save anything before. Now it does, and it counts in yesterday's set

**New in 2.1:**

Small one, but a useful one.

- **Change the dose** - say your doctor changes your dose. Tap the medication under Medications on the Health page and press **Change the dose**: set what you take now in the morning, at night and in any of your own groups (half a tablet at a time, 0 = not then), and save. From then on the **Take** and **Take all** buttons log the new amount - the old doses don't count anymore. Undo puts it back if you messed up

**New in 2.0:**

Alright, so this one's a big one - the widgets, the pills and a couple of things for the bills and all.

- **Set date on a bill** - tap a bill you haven't paid yet and there's a blue **Set date** next to Paid. Say it was supposed to come out on the 16th but it's really coming out on the 18th or 19th - pick the day, press Set date, and it moves there and stays unpaid, so Coming up and your lowest point stay accurate. **Paid is red now**, because it's money going out
- **Your own medication groups** - Morning and Night are still there, but now you can make your own: **+ New group** in the + sheet, give it a name, pick Day, Night or Any time, and pick its medications and how much. Say you take something three times a day - morning, afternoon and night, each its own group. Every group gets its own **Take all / Take the rest**, and the ✎ on any group lets you put a medication in or take one out. The Take all buttons are **silver** now
- **An Investments widget** - your total, its line over time, what your holdings made, every holding with its shares and price. And it's **one widget, any account**: put as many copies on your home screen as you want, each one asks which account it shows (your TD account, a TFSA, crypto, whatever - or all of them added up). Change it later with the **⇄ account** chip or hold the widget
- **The widgets keep up by themselves** - they check every so often and update when your files change, and they got a new look and all
- **Real landscape** - on a wide screen held sideways it goes two columns instead of one skinny one
- **Ready for any phone** - the widgets say Main account and Import, and a phone with no investments just says so

**Ratio Vitae 1.0 - the first public version.**

Your money and your health in one app, on your phone - no
account, no ads, free. It starts empty: the Budget page welcomes you, you
type your main account and what it has, and everything else (bills, cards,
categories, investments, medications) you add as you go with **+**.

- **Budget** - left now, bills coming up with your lowest point, I spent /
  money in / move money / card payments / cash / borrowing, one-tap new
  month, subscriptions, grocery list, PDF of the month
- **Stats** - your own categories and subcategories by month, quarter, year
- **Credit** - scores, cards, loans (principal and interest), payoff dates,
  buy now / pay later
- **Invest** - net worth over time, any investment account, crypto, goals,
  optional TD holdings import
- **Health** - medications as morning and night sets with Take all,
  injections, refills, weight, blood pressure, temperature, Health PDF
- Two scrolling home-screen widgets, notifications for bills and pills,
  receipt photos and card statements read for you

Install: download the APK below, open it, allow the install, then allow
**All files access** when Ratio Vitae asks (its files live in a folder called
`Ratio Vitae` on your phone - they are yours).
