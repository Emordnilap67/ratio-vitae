**Ratio Vitae 1.0 - the first public version.**

Your money and your health in one app, on your phone - no internet, no
account, no ads, free. It starts empty: the Budget page welcomes you, you
type your main account and what it has, and everything else (bills, cards,
categories, investments, medications) you add as you go with **+**.

- **Budget** - left now, bills coming up with your lowest point, I spent /
  money in / move money / card payments / cash / borrowing, one-tap new
  month, subscriptions, grocery list, PDF of the month
- **Stats** - your own categories and subcategories by month, quarter, year
- **Credit** - scores, cards, loans (principal and interest), payoff dates,
  buy now / pay later
- **Invest** - net worth over time, any investment account, crypto, goals,
  optional TD holdings import
- **Health** - medications as morning and night sets with Take all,
  injections, refills, weight, blood pressure, temperature, Health PDF
- Two scrolling home-screen widgets, notifications for bills and pills,
  receipt photos and card statements read for you

Install: download the APK below, open it, allow the install, then allow
**All files access** when Ratio Vitae asks (its files live in a folder called
`Ratio Vitae` on your phone - they are yours).
